Gap statement (candidate)

  • SE guidance already asks for model versions, prompts, session traces and human validation of LLM outputs ([baltes-2025-llm-guidelines-ese]). For agentic studies it recommends reporting human approval and rejection decisions ([llmguidelines-2026-session-traces]). The gap is therefore not disclosure itself.
  • Process-level governance exists only stage by stage: SLRs ([kitchenham-2026-guest-genai-slr-guidelines]), MSR ([de-martino-2025-primes2-llm-msr]) and thematic analysis ([jeldtoft-2026-auditable-llm-ta-workflow]). Whole-workflow gated provenance exists only outside ESE, in 2026 preprints ([zhu-2026-hler-human-oversight], [krebs-2026-fai2r]).
  • No ESE source specifies delegated decisions (which decision, to what, accepted by whom), authority bounds or human semantic gates (what is checked, against what criteria, where in the lifecycle) as reportable, reviewable provenance. The closest are [prahl-2025-arc], which covers one decision in qualitative coding, and [lin-2026-llm-se-security-assurance-survey], which names "agent authority" for evaluating agents rather than for research.
  • Logging alone is not the answer: richer traces raise overseers' confidence without raising their accuracy ([grundemclaughlin-2026-overseeing-agents]). That is why gates must be semantic and designed in advance.
  • Candidate sentence: Current SE guidance asks for prompts, traces and human validation, but treats them as per-study disclosures; it does not specify delegated decisions, authority bounds or human semantic gates as reportable, reviewable provenance across the empirical lifecycle.
